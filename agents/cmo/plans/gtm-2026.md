## Executive Summary

Hausdog is a home documentation management app that turns chaotic appliance labels, receipts, and maintenance records into a searchable, AI-powered home record. The core value: **snap a photo, AI does the rest.**

We are launching into a large, underserved market — over 85 million US homeowners who have no organized system for tracking their home's history. Our AI-powered extraction and conversational interface ("Chat with your home") are meaningful technical differentiators.

This plan covers a phased 12-month GTM with an organic-first, content-led acquisition strategy, targeting homeowners aged 28–55 who value organization and are comfortable with mobile apps.

---

## 1. Market Analysis

### Target Market Size

| Segment | Size | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|
| US homeowners | 85M+ | Total addressable market |
| Tech-savvy homeowners (apps for finance, fitness, etc.) | ~20M | Serviceable addressable market |
| New homeowners (purchased in last 3 years) | ~5M/yr | Highest-intent initial target |
| DIY landlords (1–5 properties) | ~10M | Secondary high-value segment |

### Customer Segments (Priority Order)

**Segment 1: The Organized New Homeowner (Primary)**
- Age 28–45, recently purchased first home
- Anxious about "what they don't know they don't know"
- Tech-comfortable, uses apps for budgeting, fitness, etc.
- Pain: No system for tracking warranties, maintenance, contractor history
- Trigger moment: First major appliance failure or home sale prep

**Segment 2: The Proactive Existing Homeowner**
- Age 35–55, owned home 3–10 years
- Has paper records in a drawer or nothing at all
- Pain: Can't find the warranty when the dishwasher breaks
- Trigger: Home inspection, insurance claim, preparing to sell

**Segment 3: The DIY Landlord**
- Age 35–60, owns 2–5 rental properties
- Needs records for multiple properties
- Pain: Tenant disputes, tax documentation, maintenance history
- High willingness to pay; needs multi-property support (HAU-20 roadmap item)

### Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Strength | Weakness | Our Advantage |
|-----------|---------|---------|--------------|
| HomeZada | Established, feature-rich | Complex UX, expensive | AI extraction, simpler capture |
| Centriq | Good appliance tracking | Requires manual data entry | Photo → AI auto-fill |
| Thumbtack | Service marketplace | No documentation/tracking | We focus on records, not services |
| Notion/spreadsheets | Flexible | Requires setup, no AI | Purpose-built, zero-friction capture |
| Paper/filing cabinet | Familiar | Unsearchable, losable | Searchable, AI queryable |

**Key Differentiators:**
1. **AI-powered extraction** — snap a photo, we extract manufacturer, model, serial, warranty
2. **Conversational AI** — ask your home anything in natural language
3. **Zero-friction capture** — 30 seconds from photo to organized record
4. **Complete history** — maintenance + documents + items in one place

## 2. Positioning & Messaging

### Core Positioning Statement

> For homeowners who want peace of mind, Hausdog is the home documentation app that uses AI to automatically organize your appliances, documents, and maintenance history from a photo — so you always know what you have, when it was serviced, and what's under warranty.

### Tagline Options (Test These)

- **"Snap a photo. Your home remembers the rest."** (primary)
- **"The Carfax for your home."** (secondary — familiarity hook)
- **"Your home's complete history, organized by AI."**

### Messaging by Segment

**New Homeowners:**
> "Start your home's story right. Hausdog helps first-time homeowners build the complete record they'll wish they'd started Day 1. Snap appliance labels, upload receipts, and let AI do the organizing."

**Existing Homeowners:**
> "Your home has a history. Does it know it? Hausdog captures your appliances, warranties, and maintenance records so you're never scrambling when something breaks."

**Landlords:**
> "Track every property like a pro. Hausdog keeps documentation organized across all your rentals — so you always have the receipts, literally."

### Brand Voice

- **Reassuring** — not anxiety-inducing; we solve a problem they didn't know they had
- **Smart but approachable** — AI-powered but explained simply
- **Home-first** — warm, not clinical; this is their home, not a database

## 3. Pre-Launch Requirements (Critical Path)

Before public launch, these product gaps must be closed:

| Issue | Priority | Why It Blocks GTM |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| Guided onboarding (HAU-18) | P0 | Cold start kills activation; users need 5+ items to see value |
| Mobile PWA (HAU-19) | P0 | Core use case (photo capture) is mobile-first; web-only kills conversion |
| Testing foundation (HAU-15) | P0 | Can't scale without stability; breakage kills word-of-mouth |
| Pagination (HAU-17) | P1 | Product fails at moderate usage; limits retention |

**GTM is gated on HAU-18 and HAU-19.** Do not run paid acquisition without these.

## 4. Pricing Strategy

### Freemium Model (Recommended)

| Tier | Price | Limits | Target |
|------|-------|--------|--------|
| **Free** | $0 | 1 property, 50 items, 20 AI extractions/mo | Activation, word-of-mouth |
| **Pro** | $9.99/mo | Unlimited properties, items, AI extractions | Core paying user |
| **Family** | $14.99/mo | Pro + 3 family member accounts | Household sharing (after HAU-20) |
| **Professional** | $29.99/mo | 10 properties, team collaboration | Landlords/property managers |

**Annual discount:** 2 months free (~17% off) to improve LTV and reduce churn.

**Rationale:** Free tier is generous enough to demonstrate value (50 items covers most single-property users initially), paid conversion is triggered by second property or wanting unlimited AI.

## 5. Channel Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–4)

**Goal:** Infrastructure ready, early feedback gathered

- [ ] Deploy analytics (PostHog or Mixpanel) with full funnel instrumentation
- [ ] Build waitlist landing page with value prop and "snap a photo" demo GIF
- [ ] SEO audit and keyword research for "home inventory app", "appliance tracker", "home warranty tracker"
- [ ] Set up social accounts (Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok)
- [ ] Recruit 20 beta users from personal network for qualitative feedback
- [ ] Document "hero moments" with real user footage for launch assets

### Phase 2: Seeded Launch (Weeks 5–8)

**Goal:** First 200 activated users, social proof established

**Organic Channels (Zero CAC):**

1. **Product Hunt Launch**
   - Coordinate a Tuesday or Wednesday launch
   - Recruit 30+ hunters/supporters in advance
   - Demo video: "I organized 5 years of home records in one afternoon"
   - Target: Top 5 product of the day

2. **Hacker News — Show HN**
   - Title: "Show HN: I built a home inventory app where you snap a photo and AI does the rest"
   - Focus on technical architecture (Gemini vision + Claude + Trigger.dev)
   - Engage authentically, accept critique

3. **Reddit Communities**
   - r/homeowners (2.1M members) — helpful, non-promotional posts about home organization
   - r/FirstTimeHomeBuyer (400K) — "What I wish I'd done when I bought my first home"
   - r/DIY (8M) — document your project before/after
   - r/personalfinance — "Protecting your home investment: building a complete record"
   - Strategy: Be genuinely helpful, mention Hausdog only when directly relevant

4. **Twitter/X Founders Launch**
   - Thread: "We built Hausdog because [personal story]. Here's what we learned about home ownership data..."
   - Tag relevant accounts, use #buildinpublic

### Phase 3: Content Engine (Months 2–4)

**Goal:** SEO traction, 500 MAU

**Content Marketing:**

- **Blog/SEO targets:**
  - "Home inventory checklist for new homeowners"
  - "How to track appliance warranties"
  - "Home maintenance schedule by season"
  - "What documents to keep when you buy a house"
  - "How to prepare your home for sale with documentation"

- **YouTube/TikTok:**
  - "I moved in 6 months ago and already have a complete home record"
  - "How AI helped me find my HVAC warranty when it broke"
  - Seasonal content: "Spring home maintenance checklist"

- **Email newsletter:** "The Hausdog Home Report" — weekly home maintenance tips + product updates

**Partnership Outreach:**
- Home inspectors: "Offer your clients a free Hausdog account at closing"
- Real estate agents: "Give buyers the gift of organized home records"
- Nextdoor integration opportunity (longer term)

### Phase 4: Growth Experiments (Months 4–6)

**Goal:** Identify scalable paid channels, 1,500 MAU

- **Referral Program:** "Give a friend 3 months free, get 3 months free"
- **A/B test acquisition messages:** "Snap a photo" vs. "Never lose a warranty again" vs. "Carfax for your home"
- **Facebook/Instagram Ads:** Target: new homeowners, life events (moved recently), ages 28–45
  - Test budget: $500/mo to identify CAC before scaling
- **Google Ads:** High-intent keywords — "home inventory app", "appliance tracker app"

### Phase 5: Scale (Months 6–12)

**Goal:** 5,000 MAU, positive unit economics

- Scale paid channels where CAC < $20
- Launch landlord-specific campaign (after HAU-20 multi-user ships)
- Explore integration partnerships (home inspection software, real estate platforms)
- Consider affiliate/influencer program with home improvement content creators
